Emirates

Emirates is the largest airline in the Middle East, operating over 3,600 weekly flights from its hub at Dubai International Airport. With its renouned fleet of planes featuring many Airbus A380's and Boeing 777's.

Air New Zealand

Air New Zealand is the flag carrier of New Zealand. It provides domestic and international passenger and cargo services. Known for its award-winning service, the airline operates over 400 daily flights and connecting with 49 global destinations.

Qatar

Qatar Airways is the state-owned national carrier of Qatar, established in 1993 and based in Doha. Known as a premier 5-star airline, it flies to over 170 destinations globally using a modern fleet of over 200 aircraft.

Korean Airlines

Korean Airlines is the largest airline in South Korea with its main hub in Incheon International airport. A 5-star airline and founding member of the SkyTeam. Offering premium passenger services and a top-ranked global cargo carrier.

Cathay Pacific

Cathay Pacific is the flag carrier of Hong Kong, based at Hong Kong International Airport (HKIA), providing premium passenger and cargo services to over 100 destinations worldwide. It operates a modern fleet including Airbus A350s and Boeing 777s.

Air France

Air France, founded in 1933 and headquartered in Paris, is the flag carrier of France, renowned for its French-inspired hospitality, premium service, and extensive global network. Operating primarily from Paris-Charles de Gaulle (CDG).

Delta Air Airlines

Delta Air Lines is a major American carrier and global leader, founded in 1924, headquartered in Atlanta, Georgia. It operates over 5,400 daily flights to more than 325 destinations across six continents.

American Airlines

American Airlines (AA) is one of the world's largest, 95+ year-old major carriers, offering extensive global passenger and cargo transportation. Based in Fort Worth, Texas, it operates a massive network, featuring oneworld Alliance partnerships and the AAdvantage loyalty program.

Lufthansa

Lufthansa, founded in 1953 and based in Germany, is the nation's flag carrier and one of Europe’s largest airline groups, operating as part of the Lufthansa Group. It serves major global hubs in Frankfurt and Munich, offering 4-star certified passenger service.
